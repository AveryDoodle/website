ABOUT

Graphic designer focused on branding, social media, sports graphics, and motion design.

I'm Avery McAllister, a graphic designer focused on branding, social media, sports graphics, and motion design. As a graphic designer for Concordia University Wisconsin Football, I create game-week promotions, team captain features, and player award graphics. I produce at least seven graphics per week and manage content across Instagram, Facebook, and X for a combined audience of approximately 22,000 followers.


I’m studying Communication Design with a Marketing minor at Concordia University Wisconsin, with expected graduation in May 2027. I also study through CUW’s co-op program with the Milwaukee Institute of Art & Design and play collegiate tennis. Marketing internships at OktoRocket and Mathews Company have given me experience in social media campaigns, motion content, logo updates, and print materials. My tools include Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, After Effects, Figma, Procreate, and CapCut.
